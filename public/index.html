THE LEGACY CONTINUES

Senkou Go Araki Kensha, born around 1949, was a magnificent red-coated Shiba Inu from the prestigious Araki Kensha kennel. This legendary canine was sired by the foundational dog Naka Go Akashisou and out of Nachihime Go Keiunsou of the esteemed Keiunsou line.

In the aftermath of World War II, when the Shiba Inu breed faced near extinction, Senkou emerged as a beacon of hope. Just as Senkou preserved his bloodline through dedication and resilience, our community has risen to preserve this project through the same unwavering spirit.

When the original developers abandoned this token, they thought they could destroy what we had built together. But they underestimated the power of community. Like Senkou's legacy that lives on through generations, our project continues stronger than ever through the collective will of believers.

Today, we carry forward both legacies - that of the legendary Shiba Inu and that of a community that refused to give up. Together, we're not just reviving a token; we're building a movement that honors the past while creating an unstoppable future.
